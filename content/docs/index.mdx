## Feature comparison

`✅` supported · `⚠️` partial · `❌` not supported

| | GD | IDM | NDM | Gopeed | Motrix | FDM |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HTTP / HTTPS | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| BitTorrent / Magnet | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Free | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Open source | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Browser download takeover | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Deep browser–desktop handoff | ✅ | ⚠️ | ⚠️ | ⚠️ | ❌ | ⚠️ |
| Media sniffing | ✅ | ✅ | ⚠️ | ❌ | ❌ | ⚠️ |
| HLS / m3u8 / DASH | ✅ | ❌ | ⚠️ | ❌ | ❌ | ⚠️ |
| Site-specific handling | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Extensibility (plugins) | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ | ⚠️ |

Most download managers cover the basics well. Ghost Downloader's edge is the full pipeline from resource discovery in the browser through protocol-specific handling to desktop execution — not just the download itself.

For download speed and media sniffing test data, see [Test Results](./benchmark).

Sources: [Ghost Downloader](https://github.com/XiaoYouChR/Ghost-Downloader-3), [IDM](https://support.internetdownloadmanager.com/), [NDM](https://neatdownloadmanager.com/index.php/en/), [Gopeed](https://gopeed.com/docs), [Motrix](https://motrix.app/features), [FDM](https://www.freedownloadmanager.org/).
