The gap depends on where the bottleneck is. When the server saturates the gigabit link, everyone is close (Qt and Anaconda: only 1.08–1.14× faster). When the server or route is the constraint, the difference shows up: QGIS 550 MB at equal 32 connections, IDM 18 min, GD 6 min 14 s — roughly 2.95×.

The OneDrive row used 128 connections for GD and Motrix Next vs 32 for IDM and Gopeed, so it is excluded from conclusions. GD vs Motrix Next on that row is like-for-like: `5:59` vs `11:35`.

GD lost one: QQ 9.9.32, Motrix Next `3.400`s vs GD `3.567`s, 0.167 s margin.

Gopeed hit anti-bot on three of six and did not complete them.
